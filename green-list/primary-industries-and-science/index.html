Other Spatial Scientist

居留路径（官方原文）

To be eligible you must be: • working for an accredited employer or have a job offer from one, and • paid at least NZD $35.00 an hour (the 2025 median wage) or above. If you meet all the visa requirements you can apply for a Straight to Residence Visa now. You can apply in New Zealand or come on a work visa and apply. You can also apply from outside New Zealand.

绿名单资格要求（官方原文）

You need a bachelor's degree at NZQCF Level 7, or a higher qualification with the minimum equivalent of 360 credits, which includes the requirements of a New Zealand major in the focus areas of computer science or geography.
